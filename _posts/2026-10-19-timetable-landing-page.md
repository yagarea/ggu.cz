title:  "A landing page for our timetable data"
author: "David"
---

The timetable data that's automatically processed and published at
[data.jr.ggu.cz](https://data.jr.ggu.cz/) finally has a landing page! It
explains what data is included, who's responsible and what sources are used in
its production.

This is a few years overdue, since the data has been used by the
[Transitous](https://transitous.org/sources/) project for over a year now, but
now the data should be more accessible.
